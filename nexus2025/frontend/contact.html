❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Who can participate in NEXUS 2025? ▼

NEXUS 2025 is open to all students currently enrolled in any recognized college or university across India. You must carry a valid college ID for on-site verification.

What is the registration deadline? ▼

Early Bird registration closes on August 31, 2025. Standard registration closes on September 8, 2025. On-spot registrations may be accepted based on seat availability.

Is the registration fee refundable? ▼

Registration fees are non-refundable. However, if you're unable to attend, you may transfer your registration to another eligible student by contacting us at least 5 days before the event.

Can I participate in multiple events? ▼

Yes! You can register for up to 3 events per participant. However, make sure there are no schedule conflicts. Refer to the schedule page to plan accordingly.

Are accommodation facilities available? ▼

Yes, limited accommodation is available in college hostels for outstation participants at an additional charge of ₹500 per night. Contact us early to reserve a spot.

How do I sponsor NEXUS 2025? ▼

We welcome corporate and startup sponsors. Email our partnerships team at sponsors@nexus2025.in or use the contact form above selecting "Sponsorship Enquiry".
